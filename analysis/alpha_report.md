Three measures, answering three different questions:

- **Excess return** — stock return minus SPY over the same window. Did we beat the market on the names we picked?
- **Jensen's alpha** — stock return minus beta x SPY return. Did we beat the market *after adjusting for the risk taken*? Our book is deliberately high-beta, so this is the stricter and more honest test.
- **Signal IC** — correlation between the composite score at entry and the realised return. Do the indicators predict anything?

## How to read this

There are **32 trades** in this sample. Statistical significance needs far more — 30+ as a bare minimum, and realistically hundreds. Beta is estimated from 60 days of returns and is itself noisy over a short period.

Every number above is therefore **descriptive, not evidence**. They describe what happened; they do not establish that the strategy works or that it does not. Treat this as the measurement framework, and judge the strategy only once the sample is large enough to carry the weight.
